## LinkedIn version (a touch more context)
> For the Convex All Gas Hackathon, I built **Attest** — an assistant that sits
> on your inbox and refuses to get you scammed.
>
> Everyone's email is full of recruiters, vendors, and invoices, and most of us
> can't tell a real message from a convincing fake. Attest verifies who actually
> sent each email using real authentication, auto-handles the ones it can trust,
> and holds anything it can't stand behind — a request for your SSN, a $5,000
> wire from an unverified sender, a "confirm your bank details" email — until you
> approve it. Approve once and it learns the rule.
>
> The decision path has no LLM in it, so it can't be persuaded by clever wording.
> It's built end-to-end on Convex (backend, live updates, auth, a public API),
> with AgentMail running the real inbox, Firecrawl checking who each sender is,
> and OpenAI reading the mail. It's also the working implementation of three
> published research papers, with 151 tests behind it.
>
> Live — sign in with any email and it'll walk you through a demo:
> https://dynamic-egret-864.convex.site
>
> #ConvexHackathon #AI #buildinpublic
